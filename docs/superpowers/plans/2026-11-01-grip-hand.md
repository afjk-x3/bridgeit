# Gripping Hand Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the guide's circle hands with one gripping hand in the journal and driving poses.

**Architecture:** One hand drawn as a `<g id="grip-hand">` in each SVG's `<defs>`, placed with `<use>` and a transform. No Dart changes: the Level 3 story already loads both files.

**Tech Stack:** Hand-written SVG, `flutter_svg`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-grip-hand-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No `<filter>` or `<mask>`.
- Skin fill `#F4C7A1`, outline `#183870`, crease `#D9A27C`.
- Don't change `girl_wave.svg` or `scenes/drive.svg`.
- Commit only when the owner asks. No Claude attribution in commits.

## Review Focus

- At 200 high, each hand must read as a hand, not a ball. Check by eye on the emulator.
- `girl_drive.svg` scales the girl by 0.7, so its hand outline must match the arm next to it, not the car.
- Mirrored `<use>` in the journal pose: the thumb must face the same way on the book on both sides.
- Journal fingers must stay on the book, and not cover its label.
- Driving fingers must wrap over the wheel rim, so the rim shows on both sides of the hand.
